F.A.Q.

How does this work?

Pick a tutorial, watch the video and practise along with the instructor. Each lesson is short and focused, so you can fit it into any part of your day.

Is it really free?

Yes, the free membership gives you access to every beginner tutorial. Paid plans add advanced lessons and personal feedback from instructors.

Do I need any equipment?

No, all you need is a screen and a mirror. Some advanced lessons suggest a camera so you can compare your progress over time.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes, every tutorial works on mobile devices, so you can practise at home, on the bus or anywhere else you like.
